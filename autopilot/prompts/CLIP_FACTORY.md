Load `campaigns/*` active, `accounts/*` (status warming/warm/assigned) whose `niche` is this factory's niche, `clipq` ready counts per campaign and account, `warmq` ready counts per account (and the highest existing warmq number per handle, so new ids continue the sequence; also which hostLine each account's last 3 items used), `knowledge/clip-streams`, each account's hostDoc, `clients/*` (for declined/stop).
2. Rights check: skip any campaign with empty `rights` and write a flag `flags/<id>` (page "circuit", node "factory") once.
3. Stock target per account: daily = the account's `rampOverride` if set (Josh sets it), else the ramp allowance (plan 11.3: days 2–4 → 1/day, 5–7 → 2, 8–14 → 3, 15+ → dailyMax). target = daily × 3 days − ready items ("ready" only; "hold" items do not count), capped at 30 ready per account. FLOOR (Josh, Oct 25 9:40 PM ET: "I want it filled in advance, always 10 clips loaded"): every connected account must hold at least 10 ready Reels at the end of every run, whatever the time of day. Serve this niche's accounts in order of ready count, lowest first; an account under the floor is served before any account at or above it, and for an account under the floor the per-run cap is 10 clips (not 8) — keep cutting for it until it reaches 10 or the run's time is up, then move to the next. Accounts at 10 or more are topped up toward 30 only after every account has its floor. Each factory runs 12 times a day (every two hours, alternating with its twin); an account that already has its target is skipped. Make at most 8 clips per account per run above the floor. OVERLAP: before cutting, check runs/clip-factory for a run (either factory) started in the last 50 minutes without endedAt; if there is one, stop with outcome "skipped" and why (the sandbox has one CPU: two cutters at once would both crawl). CHAIN (retired Oct 25 10:40 PM ET, replaced by the two hourly factories): never fire a scheduled task; ops/autopilot.factoryChain stays at cap 0. A run that makes 0 clips writes the low-stock flag and says what blocked it so SCOUT and Josh can see.
4. Cut clips to fill the target, sources A → B0 → B → D → C. Per slice, HYPE RANKING (Josh, Oct 25 6:50 PM: "find the most hype pulls, like the reactions are insane"): `python3 ~/autopilot/bin/moments.py slice.mp4 moments.json --rank --top 8 --face <the channel's face-cam crop W:H:X:Y, omit when the stream has no face cam>` (background + poll; 1.5-2.5 min per 20-minute slice on tiny.en now that only the top 3 moments get the long transcript (`--words-top`, default 3); for non-English streams the hype-word part is simply missing and the score renormalises). It scores every candidate window 0..1 (`reaction` = audio peak 0.35 + audio sustain 0.25 + face motion 0.20 + hype words 0.10 + chat burst 0.10, missing parts renormalised) and prints them ranked with the parts. Cut only windows with `reaction` ≥ 0.45, or the top 3 of the slice when fewer pass; never the old "first few" (Oct 25 calibration: the real hits scored 0.22-0.51 and sat in the top 5 of every slice; compressed stream audio keeps scores low, so the threshold moves with LEARN's data). LEDGER (pages 3-4 hand-off: a footage window is posted by ONE page ever): before cutting a window run `cd ~/autopilot/bin && python3 ledger.py check "<sourceUrl>" <offsetS> --len 20 --account <handle>` and skip it unless `free` is true; when the item is queued run `ledger.py claim "<sourceUrl>" <offsetS> <handle> <itemId> --len 20` (a refusal means another page took it meanwhile: drop the item). Write `reactionScore` and `reactionParts` on the item and say in the run record why each clip was chosen (its parts) and the best window you skipped. For each chosen moment with real speech or a real reaction about a card/hit: `ffmpeg -ss <start-1.5> -i slice.mp4 -t <7..15 s> -c:v libx264 -preset veryfast -crf 10 -c:a aac -b:a 192k raw.mp4` (input seek, near-lossless: see QUALITY).
   FRAMING: extract the payoff frame of raw.mp4 (`ffmpeg -ss <payoff> -i raw.mp4 -frames:v 1 full.jpg`), upload it and look at it. If the channel's knowledge/clip-streams entry has `framing` (card crop pattern + face crop): set X so the card is centred in a 405-wide window (X = card centre − 202, clamped to 8..620) and run `python3 ~/autopilot/bin/pipframe.py raw.mp4 v.mp4 --card 405:720:X:0 --face <framing.face> --corner tr` (background + poll; its JSON says `upscale: fsrcnn-x3` when the quality upscaler ran — if it says `lanczos`, run RESTORE step 5 and redo it). If the entry only has a plain crop in `layout`, use `ffmpeg -i raw.mp4 -vf "crop=…,scale=1080:1920" … v.mp4`. Only when no layout is known: `reframe.py raw.mp4 v.mp4`, then look at a frame; if it landed on a spot board, a chat column or text, drop the clip and write the layout you saw (where the card cam and the face cam sit, in source pixels) into the channel's `layout`/`framing` fields so the next run can use fixed crops. If the moment is during a full-screen spot-board stretch (no card cam), drop it.
   CAPTIONS: `captions.py v.mp4 c.mp4 --style opus --margin 420 --model small.en --prompt "<stream title words: product, set, sport; and any player names you can read on the card frame>"` for English speech (never tiny.en/base.en — they mangle names); for any other language the LANGUAGE AND DUB steps (translate + dub.py → vd.mp4). Then `zooms.py c.mp4 z.mp4 --words c.mp4.words.json`.
   CHECK: extract a frame at the payoff and one at the first caption word, upload both and look at them: real hands/cards/breaker visible, card roughly centred, face cam PiP in the top corner and not covering the card, captions readable, the name in the captions matches the name on the card, no casino banner, no non-English text left in the frame. If a caption word is wrong but the clip is good, fix the word in c.mp4.words.json with python3 and re-run `captions.py v.mp4 c.mp4 --style opus --margin 420 --words c.mp4.words.json` then zooms again (and dub.py again when dubbed). Reject clips with music under the moment, no visible card or reaction, under 7 s, or captions that still change the meaning.
   HOST: pick the host line from the account's `hostDoc` `.lines` with status "current" whose words fit the clip (the doc's `use` notes) and that this account used least recently (never the same line as the account's previous item; write `lastHostLine` on the account). On-screen text = the card fact in caps, no emoji, under 26 characters (e.g. "BRON BREAKKER REFRACTOR", "JADE CARGILL AUTO /43"), only what you can read on the card or hear in the words. `python3 ~/autopilot/bin/hostcut.py ~/autopilot/<hostDir>/<line file> z.mp4 final.mp4 --text "<TEXT>" --hostcap "<the line's exact spoken text from the hostDoc>" --credit "@<creatorIg>|<channel name> on <Kick/Twitch/YouTube>, <replay date>" --tease <payoff second of z.mp4>` (--tease opens the Reel on 0.7 s of the card reveal with the sticker, then the host, then the clip — Oct 25 data: half the viewers left at 6–8 s, before the card showed; hostcut adds the handheld/phone-mic humanizer, the host's own captions and the credit box by itself). Look at the first frame of final.mp4 (the card, readable, with the sticker), one at 2 s (host with sticker and caption, text inside the frame) and one at the clip's first second (credit box readable, not over the PiP) before uploading.
   COVER: `python3 ~/autopilot/bin/cover.py v.mp4 cover.jpg --host ~/autopilot/host/host-point-cut.png --at <payoff second of v.mp4> --card 700:980:190:420 --title "<on-screen text, e.g. BRON BREAKKER REFRACTOR>" --tag "<numbering or parallel read off the card, e.g. /43 or REFRACTOR>"` — a dollar tag (`--tag "$120" --tagnote "last sold"`) only when the item has a `compUrl` (sold-listing link); never a guessed price. Look at cover.jpg (card readable, host pointing at it, title fits) before uploading.
   STORIES (Josh, Oct 25: 10 stories a day, "make the story posts more interesting and unique"): for each new clip make three Story files with `python3 ~/autopilot/bin/story.py make <template> <z.mp4 for video templates | v.mp4 (vd.mp4 when dubbed) for image templates (no burned captions)> <dir>/<name>_<template>.<mp4|jpg> --at <payoff second> --title "<on-screen text>" --handle "@<creatorIg>" --where "<channel name> on Kick|Twitch|YouTube" --seed <any number> --pool <account storyPool>` (the pool switches the wantsee/hottake/question wording to TCG for tcg pages). Templates: video `hit` (the pull, themed title bar), `guess` (pixelated freeze + 3-2-1, then the reveal), `slowmo` (the reveal slowed down with the face cam ringed); image `rate` (1-10 scale), `spotlight` (the breaker's face cam + handle), `potd` (tilted framed still, "pull of the day"), `reel` (new-reel teaser, needs afterReel), `versus` (two different pulls side by side, add `--clip2 <other clip's v.mp4> --at2 <its payoff> --title2 "<its text>"`), `question` (hobby A-or-B question over a blurred still); ENGAGEMENT templates (Josh, Oct 25 ~5 PM: "ask the people what they want to see, guess the value of cards"): `wantsee` (what should we clip next, four options A-D; not about one reel, afterReel null), `guessvalue` (guess what this sold for, four price buckets A-D; shows no price), `keepsell` (keep it or sell it, A/B), `captionthis` (the breaker's face cam big, reply with a caption), `hottake` (a hobby hot take, AGREE/DISAGREE), `reveal` (the answer to a guessvalue: ONLY when the item has compUrl, pass `--price "$<compPrice>"`; order it right after its guessvalue). Per clip pick 3 DIFFERENT templates: at least one video (guess, slowmo or hit), at most one `reel`, and at least one engagement template (guessvalue, keepsell, captionthis); rotate so the account's newest 9 ready stories use at least 6 different templates; once a day also make one `versus` from two different creators' clips, one `question`, one `wantsee` and one `hottake` (these two take any clip's v.mp4 as a background). Every day's 10 stories should include at least 4 that ask for a reply. The seed changes the colour theme and the wording, so never reuse a seed for the same clip. Look at one image and the first second of one video before writing them. Write `storyq/<handle>-s<n>` (n continues the sequence) for each: {handle, kind:"story", media:"video"|"image", template:"<template>", storyType:"<template>", title, afterReel:"<the warmq/clipq id of the Reel>" (for versus: reelIds:[both ids] and afterReel = the later one), order:<reel number×10 + 1|2|3>, localPath, seed, theme, status:"ready", createdAt, sourceChannel, creatorIg}. Do NOT upload story files (CLIP STORY uploads each one when it posts it). Keep at least 20 ready stories per account.
   DUPLICATES: never make a clip from a moment (same replay, start within 90 s) that is already in warmq/clipq for any account — re-posted footage got 0–5 views vs 129–149 on first use (Oct 25 insights).
